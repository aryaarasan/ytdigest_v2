Privacy at YT_Digest

YT_Digest is an early beta service operated from India under the name YT_Digest. Contact: ytdigest1@gmail.com. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What we use

We use your Google sign-in identity to maintain your account. We store the channels and preferences you choose, your saved library, summary unlocks and reading activity, and subscription status. Browser storage supports sign-in, preferences and caching. If you enable email digests, we use your delivery address and schedule.

Summaries and questions

YT_Digest uses YouTube metadata and transcript services to help produce summaries. Transcript content and questions or conversation history you submit may be processed by our AI provider. AI output can contain errors; consult the original video when accuracy matters. We do not ask for your Google password.

Service providers

The service uses Google/Firebase for sign-in, hosting and data storage; YouTube for video metadata; Supadata for transcripts; OpenRouter for AI; Gmail for digest delivery; and Lemon Squeezy for checkout and subscription management. Payment details are entered with the payment provider. YT_Digest keeps subscription associations and event records rather than full card details.

Beta usage numbers

During the beta we record a small amount of usage on our own servers so we can decide whether to build Pro: the date your account was created, the days you open the app, how many summaries and Ask AI answers you use, whether you signed in as a guest or with Google, how often you open the Upgrade screen and from which button, and whether you join the Pro waitlist. We only look at these as totals across all users, never as a report about one person. We record no video titles or questions for this, and we use no advertising or third-party tracking tools. If you join the Pro waitlist, we will email the address on your Google account once when Pro opens. These records are kept until 90 days after the beta findings are written up, or deleted sooner with your account on request.

Your choices

You can change your saved preferences and turn off email digests in the app. Contact ytdigest1@gmail.com to request access to or correction of your account data. Deleting data held by YT_Digest does not delete data held by YouTube or your Google account. You can review Google account connections.

Deleting your account during the beta

There is no delete button in the app yet. To delete your account, email ytdigest1@gmail.com from the address on your Google account with the subject “Delete my account”. If you used guest sign-in, tell us roughly when you started and what you saved so we can find the account. We will confirm ownership, delete your account and its YT_Digest data from the live service, and reply when it is done. Copies in our backups expire on their own. Deleting YT_Digest data does not delete anything held by YouTube or your Google account.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists, until you ask us to delete it. Beta usage numbers are kept as described above. We have not yet set fixed retention periods for other data such as shared summary caches, operational logs and backups; this page will be updated when we do. If you ever bought a subscription, the payment provider keeps its own records under its policies.
