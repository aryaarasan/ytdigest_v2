Using the service

Summaries and availability

Summaries and answers are AI-generated aids and may contain errors. A summary may be unavailable when a suitable transcript cannot be obtained. Provider outages, unavailable videos or usage limits can affect availability. Check important details against the source video.

Subscription and billing

Checkout displays the applicable price, currency, taxes and billing interval before purchase. Recurring subscriptions renew until cancelled through subscription management. Access starts after payment verification; returning from checkout alone does not grant paid access. Cancellation normally preserves access through the remaining paid term. After expiry, paid features stop. If a failed renewal is reflected in billing records as past due or unpaid, paid Pro access is suspended immediately and returns after payment recovery is verified. Any separately valid app trial follows its own end date.

Usage limits

Free accounts get 10 summaries and 5 Ask AI answers a day. Pro accounts, including trials, get 50 summaries and 50 Ask AI answers a day. Allowances reset at 12:00 AM IST. Summaries you have already opened stay available. Requests are not unlimited.

Leaving and support

See our refund policy. To report a billing issue, contact ytdigest1@gmail.com. During the beta, account deletion is handled by email: write to ytdigest1@gmail.com from the address on your Google account with the subject “Delete my account” (details in our privacy policy). Deleting your account cancels any future renewal and ends app access immediately, including any remaining paid access, without an automatic refund.
