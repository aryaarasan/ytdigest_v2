Cancellation and refunds

YT_Digest is an early beta service operated from India under the name YT_Digest. Contact: ytdigest1@gmail.com. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Cancellation

You can manage your subscription from Subscription & billing in the app. Cancelling stops future renewals and normally preserves access until the end of the paid term. Cancellation and refund are separate actions.

Account deletion is a separate choice: it cancels future renewal and ends app access immediately, including any remaining paid term. During the beta, deletion is handled by email (see our privacy policy), and we will confirm this with you before we delete anything.

Refund policy

YT_Digest does not offer discretionary refunds. This does not override rights required by applicable law or decisions made by Lemon Squeezy, the payment provider, under its policies. YT_Digest does not provide an automatic refund-request or refund-issuance feature.

Billing problems

If you believe a payment or subscription status is incorrect, contact ytdigest1@gmail.com. Include the checkout email and an order reference if available. Never send a full card number, CVC, password or authentication code.

Provider reference: Lemon Squeezy refunds and chargebacks.
